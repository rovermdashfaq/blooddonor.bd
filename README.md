# Blood Donor BD · Android App

Native Android application for **Blood Donor BD**, built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material Design 3, Firebase Firestore, Google Sign-In (Credential Manager), and Firebase AI Logic (`gemini-3.8-flash`).

## Features
- **Verified Donor Directory & Live BD Map**: Filter voluntary donors across all 64 districts of Bangladesh and inspect interactive district pins.
- **Emergency Blood Requests**: Post urgent hospital requests, track active needs, and mark requests fulfilled or cancelled.
- **Direct Messages & Community Chat**: Real-time 1-on-1 donor coordination and community discussion room.
- **Community Feed & Success Stories**: Share posts, polls, photos, comments, and donation stories.
- **Digital Donor ID Cards, Certificates & Verification**: Role-themed donor cards, official appreciation certificates, and instant Donor/Certificate ID lookup.
- **Donation Tracking & Tools**: 90-day safe recovery countdown, eligibility quiz, blood compatibility explorer, blood bank finder, and volume/cost estimator.
- **Biometric App Lock**: Device biometric protection for personal donor records.
- **Blood Donor BD AI Assistant**: Streaming Bengali & English blood donation guide powered by Firebase AI Logic.
- **Admin Panel**: Donor verification, donation proof review, certificate issuance, notices, report moderation, and role management.
